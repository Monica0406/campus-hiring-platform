# Problem Statement

## 1. Title

Internship & Campus Hiring Platform

## 2. Domain

HRTech / Campus Recruitment

## 3. User Types

### Student

Students can create and manage their profiles, upload resumes,
view placement drives, apply for eligible opportunities, and
track their application progress.

### Company

Companies can manage their company profiles, create placement
drives, view student applications, and manage the hiring process.

## 4. Problem

Campus recruitment activities are often managed using spreadsheets,
emails, forms, and other disconnected tools. This makes it difficult
for students to discover opportunities, apply for suitable drives,
and track their application status.

Companies also need a centralized system to manage placement drives
and student applications efficiently.

## 5. Proposed Solution

The Internship & Campus Hiring Platform provides a centralized
platform for managing campus recruitment activities.

The system will provide:

- Student registration and login
- Company registration and login
- Student profile management
- Resume upload
- Placement drive management
- Student applications
- Application status tracking
- Interview management
- Offer management
- Role-based access

## 6. Core Entities

The main entities of the system are:

- Student
- Company
- Drive
- Application
- Interview
- Offer

## 7. Roles & Permissions

### Student

Students can:

- Register and log in
- Manage their profile
- Upload their resume
- View available placement drives
- Apply for eligible placement opportunities
- Track application status
- View interview information
- View offer information

### Company

Companies can:

- Register and log in
- Manage their company profile
- Create and manage placement drives
- View student applications
- Manage interview information
- Manage offer information

## 8. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- Students can register and securely log in.
- Companies can register and securely log in.
- Students can manage their profiles.
- Students can upload resumes.
- Companies can manage placement drives.
- Students can apply for eligible opportunities.
- Application information is stored consistently.
- Users can access functionality according to their roles.

## 9. Out of Scope

The initial MVP does not include:

- Online payment processing
- Real-time chat
- Video interviews
- Advanced AI-based candidate matching
- External job-board integrations
- Production-scale notification infrastructure

These may be considered as future enhancements.
